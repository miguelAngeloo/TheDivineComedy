# The Divine Comedy — Lore

*Skyrim, 4E 205. A province caught outside of time.*

---

## Timeline

- **4E 201** — Alduin returns. The civil war splits Skyrim. The Last Dragonborn
  shouts the World-Eater from the sky.
- **4E 203** — **The Silence.** A Dragon Break tears through the province. The
  living vanish.
- **4E 205** — *The present.* You arrive among the Waiting.

---

## The Silence

In **4E 201**, the Dragonborn shouted Alduin from the sky. But the Thu'um bends
time, and Alduin *was* the ending of time — and so much power gathered in one
place left a wound.

In **4E 203**, that wound opened: a **Dragon Break**, a rupture where past,
present, and what-comes-after fold together. When it settled, Skyrim was
**empty**. Not destroyed — emptied. The cities still stand, cold and whole. The
forges wait. No war took the living, no plague. **Time took them.**

---

## The Waiting

You are not from here. You arrived — stepped into this broken province from
somewhere else, or somewhen else — and found it silent.

You do not hunger as the living hunger. You do not age. And when your body
fails, you do not end: your soul is pulled to the nearest **temple** and
reshaped there, whole again. The old texts call your kind the **Waiting** —
souls caught in the fold of the Break, neither returned to the world that was,
nor passed on to what lies beyond.

Why you are here, the gods will not say.

---

## The Temples

When the Divines withdrew from the broken world, they left the consecrated
ground behind as the last thread between the Waiting and the beyond. That is why
the temples reclaim your soul when you fall — Talos in Windhelm, Mara in Riften,
Kynareth in Whiterun, the Divines in Solitude, Dibella in Markarth. They are the
only mercy left in a province that has none.

---

## The Long Climb

This place has taken the shape of an old poem — Inferno, Purgatorio, Paradiso.

You began in the **Inferno**: alone, scavenging a dead province. What you build
now is the **Purgatory** — the slow climb back toward a living world, stone by
stone, ingot by ingot. Not by a hero with a prophecy, but by workers: hands that
mine, forge, weave, and trade. There are no Jarls left to serve, no quests handed
down. Only each other.

And the old texts promise a **Paradiso**. They say a soul who has built enough,
and belonged enough, earns the **True Death** — not an ending, but a rising. The
passage the Break sealed, opening at last. No one has proven it. But every stone
you lay is a step toward it.

---

## Mechanics ↔ Lore

- **The world is empty** — the Dragon Break of 4E 203 took the living.
- **You respawn at temples** — only consecrated ground can reshape a soul.
- **Chests are bare, but the earth still gives** — no world to loot, only honest
  work: the mine, the forge, the loom.
- **You rebuild a society** — that is the climb out of the Inferno.
- **The reward is the True Death** — the rumored rising to the Paradiso.

Welcome to the Silence, 4E 205. Will you climb?
